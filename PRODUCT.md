# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Vietnamese Gen Z young adults (18–28), primarily Android, tracking daily habits in a gamified context. Also English-speaking users internationally. Mobile-first, short sessions. Core job: log today's habits, see rank progress, stay motivated to keep the streak alive.

## Product Purpose

Habi is a gamified habit tracker. Users log activities, earn rank points, climb a weekly leaderboard, and grow a self-treat fund (in VND). Success = a user opening the app daily without friction, feeling proud of their streak, and anticipating their next rank-up.

## Brand Personality

Sharp · Focused · Minimal — but not cold. The green primary and gold star accents carry warmth; minimal means purposeful, not sterile.

## Anti-references

- **Notion**: too cold, monochrome, text-heavy. Habi must feel alive and personal.
- **Fintech dashboards**: no navy/gold palette, no corporate chart-overload, no dense data grids.

## Design Principles

1. **Earn your delight** — celebration animations and rewards fire only for real achievements (rank-up, streak milestone), never as decoration.
2. **One thing at a time** — each screen has one primary action. Secondary info is always subordinate.
3. **Sharp, not sterile** — minimal means no noise, not no warmth. Color, type weight, and spacing carry personality.
4. **Vietnamese-first, globally legible** — copy is Vietnamese by default; the EN translation is a first-class citizen, not an afterthought.
5. **Mobile precision** — 44pt touch targets, safe areas, one-thumb reachability. Every interaction is thumb-first.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum throughout. Support for system reduceMotion (all looping animations gated). Proper accessibilityRole, accessibilityLabel on all interactive elements. Minimum font size 11sp for decorative chips, 12sp for body-adjacent text. Dark mode fully supported via theme tokens.

Privacy Policy - Habi

Last updated: August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Habi mobile application (Google Play package name com.habitring.app), published on Google Play by Thanh Nguyen Xuan, an individual developer operating under the Google Play developer account thanhnguyenxuan970. This policy is written to match the app name and developer identity shown on Habi's Google Play Store listing.

Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, the App collects:

  • Your Google account display name
  • Your Google account email address
  • Your Google account profile photo URL

When you use the app, Habi also stores the habit and activity data you create yourself: tasks, completion logs, streaks, challenges, achievements, and rank/leaderboard progress.

Habi does not collect location data, contacts, photos/media (other than your Google profile photo URL), or device identifiers beyond what Google Sign-In provides.

How We Use Your Information

Your Google account information is used solely to identify your account within the App and, if you opt into the leaderboard, to display your name/photo to other users. Your habit and activity data powers the App's core features: streaks, analytics, challenges, and rank. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Where Your Data Is Stored

All habit data (tasks, streaks, activity logs) is stored locally on your device using SQLite. It is also synced to our backend (Supabase) so it can be backed up and used for cross-device sync and the global leaderboard. Access to your synced data is tied to your Google identity.

Data Retention

Local data on your device is retained for as long as the App remains installed. Server-side data (your Google profile info and synced habit history) is retained for as long as your account remains active, i.e., for as long as you keep the App installed and signed in. We do not retain data indefinitely after account deletion: deleting your account permanently removes your data from our servers immediately, rather than after a delay.

Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time:

  • In-app self-service deletion (recommended): Open Settings → Delete Account inside Habi. This permanently deletes your account data — including your Google profile link, activity history, streaks, achievements, challenges, and leaderboard entries — from both your device and our servers (Supabase). This action is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Uninstalling the app: Removes all data stored locally on your device. This does not by itself delete data already synced to our servers; use the in-app deletion above or contact us to remove server-side data.
  • Revoking Google access: You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • By email: You may also request deletion of your data by emailing thanhnguyenxuan970@gmail.com with your Google account email address.

Third-Party Services

Google Sign-In (Google LLC) is used for authentication. Google's privacy policy applies to the sign-in process: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Supabase (Supabase Inc.) is used as our backend database/hosting provider to store synced habit data and support the leaderboard. Supabase's privacy policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

We do not use these services, or any other third party, for advertising, analytics resale, or data brokerage.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated "Last updated" date.
